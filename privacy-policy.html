Privacy Policy

Cattle & Herd Records · last updated 21 September 2026

The short version

This app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Nothing you type into it is transmitted anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

Your animals, events, treatments, protocols and settings are written to storage on your own device and stay there. We cannot read them, because they never reach us.

Backups and exports

The app can write a backup file or a CSV. That file goes wherever you send it — the share sheet, Files, iCloud Drive, email. Once it leaves the app it is governed by whichever service you chose, not by us. We never see it.

Third parties

There are none. The app makes no network requests in normal use. It contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporter.

Children

The app is a livestock record book and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Settings has an option that erases every record on the device. Deleting the app also removes its storage. There is nothing held elsewhere to request the deletion of.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of the app ever transmits data, that will be stated plainly here and in the App Store privacy label before it ships.
